Downtown Palo Alto, 1900 to today

Map, open source · part of Townlapse

A satellite map with a timeline slider. Drag it and watch a century of shops, theaters and startups come and go on each block.

Neighbors suggest places they remember, and an AI checks every one against old newspapers before it goes on the map. The whole thing is a free template you can copy for your own town.
