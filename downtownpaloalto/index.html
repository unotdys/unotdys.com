    About this map

    Drag the timeline to see which shops, restaurants, theaters and offices stood in downtown Palo Alto in any year from 1900 to today. It's a community project: neighbors add the places they remember, and every suggestion is checked against historical sources before it goes on the map.

    Where the history comes from

    • Palo Alto city directories from 1904, 1924 and 1925 (Internet Archive)
    • Palo Alto telephone books and Yellow Pages, 1929–1963 (Library of Congress)
    • OpenStreetMap, for today's businesses and its year-by-year history since 2013
    • Local reporting and histories, including Palo Alto Online and the Palo Alto Historical Association
    • Memories and corrections from neighbors like you

    Years marked ~ are estimates. Directory entries show the years a business was listed, so it may have been open longer. See something wrong? Use "Suggest a fix" on any place.
